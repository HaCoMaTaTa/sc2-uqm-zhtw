Cross-race comparison revealed pkunk was the ONLY outlier using half-width
Latin punctuation inside Chinese dialog:

| Race | half `?` | full 「？」 | half `(X)` | full 「（X）」 |
|------|---------:|----------:|-----------:|-------------:|
| pkunk (before) | **93** | 4 | **32** | 6 |
| syreen | 0 | 65 | 0 | 17 |
| mycon | 0 | ... | 0 | 9 |
| melnorme | 0 | ... | 0 | 46 |
| arilou | 0 | ... | 0 | 19 |

pkunk v0.1 shipped drifted to Western punctuation conventions inside CJK
context; every other race file uses full-width Q1=B canonical.

## Normalization Applied

Dialog values only (skipped `_notes` annotations):
- 93 × `?` → `？` (66 keys touched)
- 32 × `(English...)` → `（English...）` (24+ keys touched)

Internal English punctuation preserved (e.g., 「（Whoopdy Dee and Trolly
Bazoo!）」keeps `!` half-width because it's inside English phrase).

Empty `(` `)` structural characters left untouched (no code/URL contexts
existed in pkunk).

_notes annotations restored (9 items) to keep original half-width
convention — those are internal decision comments, not shipped text.
